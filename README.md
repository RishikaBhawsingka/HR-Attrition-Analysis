
# 📊 HR Workforce & Employee Attrition Analytics

An executive-style **HR Analytics and Employee Attrition Dashboard** built using **Power BI, Python, Pandas, and DAX**.

The project transforms raw employee data into an interactive business intelligence solution that helps HR teams understand workforce composition, identify attrition patterns, analyze potential attrition drivers, and support data-driven retention decisions.

---

## 🎯 Project Overview

Employee attrition is a major business challenge because high turnover can increase recruitment costs, reduce productivity, and affect workforce stability.

This project analyzes employee-level HR data and develops an interactive Power BI dashboard focused on:

- Workforce overview
- Employee attrition
- Department-level attrition
- Job-role attrition
- Age-group trends
- Overtime impact
- Job satisfaction
- Work-life balance
- Job level
- Business travel
- HR decision support

The final solution contains **two executive-style dashboard pages** designed for quick business interpretation.

---

## 🏢 Business Objective

The primary objective is to answer questions such as:

- How many employees are currently represented in the dataset?
- What is the overall employee attrition rate?
- Which departments experience higher attrition?
- Which job roles have the highest turnover?
- Are overtime employees more likely to leave?
- Does job satisfaction relate to attrition?
- How does work-life balance relate to employee turnover?
- Does frequent business travel correspond with higher attrition?
- Which employee groups should HR prioritize for retention initiatives?

---

## 📂 Dataset

The project uses the **IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition dataset**.

### Dataset Size

- **1,470 employees**
- **35 original attributes**
- Employee-level HR information
- No missing values
- No duplicate records

### Major Dataset Categories

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Employee Information | Age, Gender, Marital Status |
| Employment | Job Role, Job Level, Department |
| Compensation | Monthly Income, Salary Hike |
| Satisfaction | Job Satisfaction, Environment Satisfaction |
| Work Conditions | Overtime, Business Travel, Work-Life Balance |
| Experience | Total Working Years, Years at Company |
| Attrition | Attrition status |
